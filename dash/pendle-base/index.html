Vault → Pendle on Base (automatic)

The vault's Base address is owned by a NEAR contract through Chain Signatures: no person holds its key. Money moves through NEAR Intents. You sign the setup once; after that the keeper runs it (vault → Base → buy PT → report). The way back stays yours.
